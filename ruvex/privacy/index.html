Privacy Policy — Ruvex

Last updated: September 22, 2026
App: Ruvex
Bundle ID: com.ruvex.zwwnoa
Contact: ruvexzwwnoa@yahoo.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Ruvex (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information when you use the iOS application. Ruvex is an on-device atelier for creating Wig Mood Cards and browsing a sample Inspiration lookbook. Please read this Policy together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Ruvex is provided for personal, non-commercial use on your Apple device. For privacy questions or reports of inappropriate activity, contact ruvexzwwnoa@yahoo.com.

2. Information we store on your device

Ruvex is designed to keep most data on your device. We do not operate a Ruvex account server that syncs your mood cards or lookbook preferences.

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be stored locally (for example in UserDefaults, app file storage, or system frameworks you authorize):

  • A locally generated display username and session flags after Quick Login
  • Agreement timestamp for the End User License Agreement (EULA)
  • Credit balance granted on first login and adjusted by in-app purchases and synthesis
  • Mood card drafts and finished cards (titles, captions, fiber/lace metadata, stickers, optional photo file references)
  • Lookbook preferences: hidden sample posts, blocked sample authors, lookbook on/off
  • Appearance mode (light / dark / system)
  • Local lookbook comment text you post on sample posts
  • Report ticket identifiers prepared when you report a sample look

We do not ask for your real name, postal address, or payment card number inside Ruvex. Apple processes payments for Credits.

3. Permissions and when they are requested

Ruvex requests system permissions only when you use the related feature:

Permission When Purpose
Camera Photo input / live silhouette capture Capture a wig silhouette for a mood card
Photo Library (read) Choosing an existing portrait Select a styling portrait for a mood card
Photo Library (add) Export Card to Photos Save a finished mood card image you choose to export
Microphone Tap to Dictate on Card Editor Convert a spoken caption into editable text
Speech Recognition Same dictation flow On-device speech-to-text for captions
App Tracking Transparency (ATT) First time you open the Inspiration (Home) tab Optional advertising identifier use as described in the system prompt
Push Notifications Immediately after the ATT prompt returns on first Home visit Authorization request only; Ruvex does not implement push campaign handlers beyond the permission request

You can change Camera, Microphone, Photos, Tracking, and Notifications in iOS Settings. Denying a permission disables that feature; the rest of the App remains available where possible.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Ruvex does not create biometric faceprints, face templates, or Face ID data. If you choose Camera or Photos, you may supply images that may depict a face or a styled silhouette. Those images are treated as ordinary user media you select for mood-card composition.

4.2 Planned use

Optional photos or camera frames are used solely to compose, preview, and archive Wig Mood Cards on your device, and—if you choose Export—to save an image to your Photo Library.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and storage location

Facial imagery you provide is stored on your device within the App’s sandbox (and in Photos only if you export). Ruvex does not upload these images to a Ruvex server. We do not sell facial imagery.

4.4 Retention

Images and card records remain until you delete the related card, use Delete Account, uninstall the App, or clear App data. Export to Photos follows Apple Photos retention under your control.

4.5 Scope of this chapter

This Section 4 (Facial data) is the explanation of collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial-related media for Guideline 2.1 / 5.1.1 review purposes.

5. Third-party AI and network processing

Mood-card “Confirm & Synthesize” rendering runs on your device. Ruvex does not send your prompts, captions, or reference images to a third-party generative AI API for synthesis.

Speech dictation uses Apple’s on-device Speech Recognition and microphone APIs while you hold the dictate control. Audio is used for live transcription and is not stored or uploaded by Ruvex as a recording archive.

StoreKit communicates with Apple to complete Credit purchases. Report actions may open the iOS Mail composer (mailto:) so a report body can leave your device toward ruvexzwwnoa@yahoo.com.

6. App Tracking Transparency

If you allow tracking when prompted on first Home, Apple may make an advertising identifier available under your iOS settings. Ruvex describes measurement of which atelier tools people open. You may deny ATT; the App continues to function.

7. Push notifications

After ATT returns on first Home, Ruvex requests notification authorization. There is no separate in-app “Enable Notifications” button. Declining notifications does not block browsing or card creation.

8. In-app purchases and Credits

Credits are a virtual currency purchased through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes payment; Ruvex never receives or stores your card number. First Quick Login grants a signup allotment of Credits. Synthesizing a mood card spends Credits on device. There is no Restore Purchases control in Ruvex.

9. Sample lookbook, comments, and moderation data

Inspiration presents a sample lookbook (seed authors and looks). It is not a live social network. Locally you may:

  • Hide / remove sample posts from your lookbook
  • Block sample authors (their sample cards leave your lookbook)
  • Post comments stored on device
  • Report a look, which prepares a mailto report (ticket id, look id, author, reason), hides the look, and blocks the author on device

Keyword filtering may block publishing comments that contain listed objectionable terms. Report email content leaves the device only if you send the mailto draft.

10. Account logout and deletion

Log Out ends the local session flags needed to re-enter Home until you Quick Login again.

Delete Account permanently clears Ruvex’s local domain data (including cards, Credits balance, lookbook preferences, comments store, and agreement flags) and returns you to Login. Cards and Credits cannot be restored after deletion. This does not delete images you previously exported to Photos, nor does it cancel Apple’s purchase records.

11. Children

Ruvex is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us so we can help you remove local data guidance.

12. Sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal information.

13. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy where permitted by law.
